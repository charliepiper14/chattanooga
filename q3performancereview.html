Q4 2026 strategy

Six rules from the data

1. Early access wins

The Black Friday teaser was last year's best Q4 result. Build an early-access list from October, and open Black Friday to them 24 to 48 hours first.

2. Fund warm audiences separately

Warm ad sets returned between 3.15x and 6.13x across the 2026 events. Give customers, carts and engaged people their own fixed budgets, and never leave them to Advantage+ to find.

3. Events, not always-on

2026 event campaigns ran at over 4x; always-on ran under 3x. Put most of Q4 spend into four windows and run a lean base in between.

4. One offer per window

The Cyber Monday BOGO after Black Friday returned 1.01x. Run Black Friday through Cyber Monday as one offer with a final-day push, not two competing sales.

5. Gifting sells December

Christmas buyers are often the wife or gift buyer. Lead with gift sets, scent guides and the shipping cutoff, and stop prospecting once delivery can't be promised.

6. Protect margin

Confirm a sale-price breakeven with Todd before each event. Scale only when ROAS clears it for 2 days running, in 20% steps.

Q4 2026 calendar

Four windows, one plan

  1. Oct 1 to 23

    Build and bank

    Lean prospecting on the best 2026 creative. Lock & Key countdown starts. Launch the early-access sign-up for Black Friday (email and text). Brief gift-guide creative.

  2. Oct 24 to 31

    Lock & Key final week, Halloween

    Lock & Key 'gone Oct 31' push to warm audiences (the DISAPPEAR video is ready). Optional Halloween flash, which returned 2.62x last year.

  3. Nov 1 to 23

    Warm up

    Grow warm pools with gift and scent content. Retarget viewers. Collect early-access sign-ups. Keep spend moderate; CPMs rise from mid-November.

  4. Nov 24 to 26

    Early access

    Black Friday opens early to the early-access list and past customers. Warm ad sets only, plus Todd's email and text.

  5. Nov 27 to 30

    Black Friday to Cyber Monday

    One offer across four days: fresh Advantage+ sales campaign, warm ABO, cart ad set, and an auto-scheduled Cyber Monday final-day ad set. Small Business Saturday (Nov 28) suits the handmade story.

  6. Dec 1 to 16

    Gifting

    Gift sets and scent guides for gift buyers. Green Monday (Dec 14) flash to warm audiences; last year's Christmas flash retargeting returned 3.54x.

  7. Dec 17 to 24

    Last shipping days

    Shipping-cutoff urgency (confirm carrier dates with Todd). Wind down prospecting after the cutoff and switch to e-gift cards if available.

  8. Dec 26 to 31

    New beards, reset

    Retarget December buyers for their own refills and new scents; set up January reactivation.
