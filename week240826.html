The rebuild is underway.
Store up 43%, structure
replaced, one break to fix.

August is the July plan being executed. We killed the two ads that were leaking, restructured the Obsidian ASC, and stood up a new evergreen campaign on Aug 15. Store gross is $11,062 across 24 days, up 43%, with orders up 49% and checkout close rate improving to 51.9%. The paid line is where a rebuild month always sits: spend moved into a campaign that is still learning, and last week reads soft at 1.35x. Some of that is real and one piece needs fixing this week. Some of it is a seven-day attribution window that has not finished filling in.

Caveat one, the window has not closed. This data was pulled Aug 24. What does survive all three caveats. Reach rose 47% while order value fell 20%, and that pairing shows up at the ad level too, in the same direction, on the one ad we scaled. Attribution lag does not move order value and neither does learning phase. That is the piece worth acting on, and it is section 06.

Frequency barely moved, so this is not fatigue. 2.11 to 2.58 is nowhere near the 3.0 flag or the 4.0 act line. The creative did not wear out. It was pushed into a reach pool four times larger and the quality of that pool collapsed. Order value dropped 41% at the same time, which is what happens when Advantage+ trades intent for volume to spend a bigger budget.

The way to fund a winner without breaking it is to isolate it in its own campaign at the daily spend it already proved, then step 20% at a time against the standing rule. Feeding it through the ASC has now failed twice in three months.

The month in one line

August did the structural work. The store grew 43% while we rebuilt underneath it. Now isolate the packing winner, clear the four soft ads, and build Churchwarden and the Labor Day flash.

Nothing here is a demand problem. Order value held at $47.67, checkout close rate improved to 51.9%, and the store cleared $11,062 in 24 days with 263 orders. The paid line reads soft because we spent the month replacing the structure that was leaking, and because a chunk of last week has not finished reporting. One real fix is on the table: the packing video needs its own campaign at the budget it proved rather than being fed through the ASC. Do that, add Churchwarden, put a flash on Labor Day weekend, and September has the pieces to run well above the floor.
