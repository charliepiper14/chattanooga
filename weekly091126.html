What last year teaches

Four rules for this Black Friday

Margin

Discount depth sets the bar

  • At 40% off, breakeven was about 6x
  • Last year made 1.71x, so the more it spent, the more it lost

Audience

Warm first, always

  • Warm teaser 7.83x, cold 1.58x
  • This year early access goes to buyers, email and warm before anyone else

Timing

The week before is the big one

  • 17 to 23 Nov did $20.3K, more than Black Friday week
  • This year that week is 16 to 22 Nov

Customers

November buyers come back

  • 100 November buyers came back in 49 later months, December buyers in 23
  • Find new people in November, sell gifts to buyers in December

Breakeven assumes 2x covers product, packing and shipping at full price. Repeat figures: Shopify cohort report, first purchases Nov 2025 to Oct 2026.

SPEAKER NOTES · 11Breakeven: if 2x is breakeven at full price, product, packing, shipping and fees take half of the full price. At 40% off, $100 of full-price product sells for $60, costs still $50, leaving $10, so breakeven ROAS is 60 / 10 = 6.0x. Cohorts: November 2025, 416 new customers, 203 repeat customer-months over the next 11 months (48.8 per 100). December 2025, 349 new customers, 79 repeat customer-months over 10 months (22.6 per 100). Shopify counts a customer once in each month they buy, so these are repeat months, not orders or unique people, and identified customers only. The point holds either way: Black Friday buyers are worth about twice Christmas buyers to the business over the year.

Keep the handwritten note, candy and sticker: they cost little and carry the brand. Say the price and the end date in the first 2 seconds of every ad. Todd sets the numbers; the structure is what matters.

Breakeven assumes 2x covers product, packing, shipping and fees at full price. Free shipping and gifts add cost on top, so treat the bars as minimums.

SPEAKER NOTES · 12Method: costs are half the full price at 2x breakeven. At discount d, revenue is (1 - d) of full price and the margin left is (1 - d) - 0.5, so breakeven ROAS = (1 - d) / ((1 - d) - 0.5). 20%: 0.8 / 0.3 = 2.67x. 25%: 0.75 / 0.25 = 3.0x. 30%: 0.7 / 0.2 = 3.5x. 40%: 0.6 / 0.1 = 6.0x. Why A: the spend-more tier pays for its deeper discount with a bigger basket. October's $13 jars bought volume but cut the average order 8%; this week's new-customer order was $39.39 at full price. The Explorer kit ($72) sits just under $75 and the bundles stack toward it. Todd wants 5x to 6x at Black Friday; the shallower the discount, the more of the budget can clear that. During the offer, the scaling gate becomes the offer's breakeven, not 2x.
