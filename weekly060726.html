03 The Scaling Lesson

Day 2 spent more and returned less

Day 1Day 2
Meta spend$239$393
Gross$4,734$3,489
Blended MER19.8x8.9x
Campaign frequency2.863.76

What happened

  • We pushed budget up on day 2 and the campaign did spend it, unlike prior flashes.
  • But the audience is small. Frequency climbed to 3.76 and efficiency halved.
  • Mirage, scaled +40%, faded on day 2 (1 sale) once its pool saturated.
Takeaway for the next flashFront-load day one, cap day-two daily spend lower, and widen the audience before scaling rather than jamming budget into a saturating pool. Reach is the ceiling here, not budget.

08 What We Do Next

Three moves, in priority

1 · Churchwarden creative

Dedicated video-led set for the proven #1 seller. Biggest untapped lever.

2 · Rebuild the evergreen layer

Between flashes the baseline is thin. Stronger always-on prospecting on the video winners so revenue does not fully depend on events.

3 · Size the next flash right

Front-load day one, cap day-two daily, widen the audience before scaling. Protects the efficiency we lost on day 2.
