What October means for Black Friday

Four things to carry into November

Offer

Say the price and the end date

  • Last call 14.40x, $13 versions 5.53x
  • Black Friday ads lead with the offer and the date, nothing clever first

Warm pool

Grow it before it is needed

  • 7,086 people saw warm 12.6 times
  • Cold Obsidian and Todd's videos add viewers now for Black Friday warm

Baskets

Protect the order value

  • Average order $38.60, down 8%. Discounts up 37%
  • Bundles or a spend-more tier beat a flat cut on every jar

New buyers

Black Friday is for finding them

  • New customers down 11% this year
  • Last November brought 410 new, and they keep coming back

Scaling rule stays: up 20% only after 3 days at 2x or better. Black Friday is Friday 27 Nov, 25 days away.

SPEAKER NOTES · 14The baskets point is a suggestion for Todd, not a finding that bundles will work: it says the $13 offer lifted orders but cut order value. Bundle Beard Balm and Bundle Beard Oil already sold 159 units in October, so there is a base to build on. Warm audiences to build this week in Meta: video viewers at 50% over 365 days, Facebook and Instagram engagers over 365 days, site visitors over 180 days, adds to cart over 30 days, and past buyers over 365 days.
