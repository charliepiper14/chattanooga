The scaling call
  • ▸No evergreen budget increase yet. Last-7 paid is at 1.62x. Todd's rule holds: nothing scales without three to four days at 2x plus. Fix the creative first, then the rule clears the increase.
  • ▸The scalable asset is the flash structure. Independence returned 5.53x. Build the next flash on the Independence Video template, fresh standalone ASC, existing-customer cap at 0%.
  • ▸Once fresh Mirage and Churchwarden hold 2x plus for three to four days, step budgets 20% at a time, penny method on the ad sets already clearing the floor.
The call

The month cleared breakeven and the store is strong. The paid engine is running on tired creative and a flash that has ended. Repair the evergreen, brief Churchwarden, and hold budgets until fresh creative earns the increase.

Now: pause the two dead ads, refresh Mirage, rebuild the starved winners, brief Churchwarden. Then: run the next flash on the Independence template and step budgets once fresh creative holds 2x plus.
