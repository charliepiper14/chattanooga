Scaling rule: budget up 20% at a time, only after 3 to 4 days at 2x or better.

Lock & Key cold has read above 2x on every check since 1 Oct: 3.92x on 1 Oct, 2.51x on 2 to 3 Oct, 4.75x on 3 to 5 Oct, 4.03x overall. That clears the 3 to 4 day rule for a 20% step. Warm frequency 4.75 in five days says the audience is close to saturated at $25.
