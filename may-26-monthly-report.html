May was the strongest revenue month on the account in the current campaign window. Shopify gross reached $19,639, up 52.9% month over month and 58.3% against the same month last year. We more than doubled Meta spend to fund two events at once: the Mirage line launch in its own standalone campaign and the Memorial Day sale. Meta ROAS settled at 3.32x, just under the 3.5x line, because spend scaled into newer audiences. The blended MER of 8.16x keeps the business well clear of breakeven, and new customer count jumped 58%.

Mirage Out of the Gate

Mirage launched May 14 in its own standalone Advantage+ campaign at $40 daily. In 17 days it generated 66 purchases and $3,052.74 at 3.43x. For a brand new scent line with no prior data, opening at breakeven is a strong start. The reads inside the set are what shape the next move.

Tropical Set carried the launch, and the risk

The S2 Tropical Set video spent $663.55, which is 74% of all Mirage spend, and returned $2,449.41 at 3.69x with 55 of the 66 purchases. It is the launch. The problem is frequency at 5.19x already. One ad doing this much work is a concentration risk that needs a second hero before scaling.

The V3 video is the hidden winner

MIRAGE V3 spent only $29.24 and returned $215.35 at 7.36x with 4 purchases and a 1.06% click rate. On small spend it is the most efficient creative in the set. This is the next ad to feed budget into so the launch stops leaning on one video.

The teasers did their job, then stopped

The three teaser ads (Mystery, Scent Drop, Founder) ran pre-launch to warm the audience. Combined they spent $50.46 for 1 purchase. That is fine for a teaser phase, but they are off now and should not draw spend going forward.

V1 is the weak hero candidate

MIRAGE V1 spent $95.50 at 1.66x with a $31.83 cost per purchase. It is the second largest spend in the set but the weakest return. Before any scale, V1 either gets a hook swap or its budget moves to V3 and S1, which both run above 3.5x.

09: The Drag

One Ad Set Held the Average Down

If Adv+ Winners had simply matched the account average, the core campaign would have landed near 4x rather than 3.26x. The drag is narrow and fixable, and it sits almost entirely in one ad.

V3 Origin Story is the lever

It took $421.71, which is 64% of the Adv+ Winners budget, and returned 2.08x with a $24.81 cost per purchase. The click rate was healthy at 1.32%, so the hook works, but those clicks are not converting at a profitable rate. Moving this budget is the single biggest improvement available in June.

The legacy video pool is tired

Founder Talk 03 at 0.61x, Founder Unbox 05 at 1.45x, and Holycow Ingredient at zero purchases are the older video ads. They are already off or near off. The set has been running on creative that has had its run, while the fresh Obsidian Scale ads sit underfunded.

The bench is ready

Wife Buyer at 9.83x, Skeptic at 22.58x, and Quiet Craftsman at 6.61x all proved out on small spend inside Obsidian. These are the ads that should inherit the budget coming off V3 Origin Story, rather than leaving it inside a tired set.

What this is not

This is not a frequency or fatigue story like last month. Adv+ Winners frequency is a moderate 3.12x. The issue is creative fit on specific ads, not audience overexposure. That points to swapping ads, not pulling spend or resting the audience.

What's happening

New customer count rose 58% month over month, from 86 to 136, the highest monthly intake in the trailing quarter. The doubled Meta spend behind the Mirage launch and Memorial Day pulled in a fresh wave of first-time buyers. Returning customers also grew in absolute terms, from 194 to 246. Both engines moved up together.

Why the returning rate dipped

The returning rate fell from 69.3% to 64.4% only because new customers grew faster than returning ones, which is what a launch month should do. A lower returning rate driven by rising new acquisition is the healthy version of that move. The cohort is feeding the top of the funnel that drives next quarter's repeat revenue.

The acquisition math:

At a $14.49 blended Meta cost per purchase against an $83 lifetime value, the CAC to LTV ratio sits near 1 to 5.7 even in a heavy-spend month. With a returning customer base this strong, every new buyer brought in during May is an asset that compounds. This is the month to keep the acquisition tap open, not tighten it.

Gross sales up 44.5% year on year

New customer growth was the slower line year on year at 7.8%. May's 58% jump in new buyers is the start of correcting that. Keeping new acquisition climbing while the returning base compounds is the path to the next step up in gross sales.

13: Diagnosis

What's Working, What Needs Work

Working: the launch playbook

Mirage opened at 3.43x in its own campaign and Memorial Day closed at 4.59x in a fresh ad set. The discipline of giving launches and sales their own structure, rather than folding them into existing ads, is producing clean reads and strong opening numbers every time it runs.

Working: the Obsidian set and the bench

Obsidian Launch ran at 4.00x with healthy frequency, and the smaller Obsidian Scale ads (Wife Buyer 9.83x, Skeptic 22.58x, Quiet Craftsman 6.61x) all proved out. There is a deep, tested bench ready to absorb budget as the tired ads come off.

Needs work: the Adv+ Winners drag

The set ran at 2.08x with a $25.26 cost per purchase, held down by V3 Origin Story taking $421.71 at 2.08x. This is the rotating constraint for May. It is a creative-fit problem on specific ads, and the fix is reallocation into the proven bench.

Needs work: Mirage frequency

The launch set is at 6.22x frequency with one video carrying 74% of spend. Before Mirage scales, it needs a second hero so the audience is not hit by the same ad over and over. The V3 video is the obvious candidate to build that depth around.

The diagnosis in one line:

A record revenue month with healthy launch economics, held back from an even better Meta number by one tired ad set and a single-creative dependency inside Mirage. Both are reallocation problems, not spend or audience problems, and both have a ready fix.

16: Budget & Scaling

The Scaling Window

Reallocate first, then scale the proven sets once efficiency returns.

May proved spend can more than double and still hold above 3x on Meta with an 8x MER underneath. The next increment is not a blanket budget rise. It is moving the V3 Origin Story budget to the bench, building Mirage's second hero, and letting the core campaign climb back toward 4x. Once Mirage holds above 3.5x for three to four days with two heroes carrying it, the launch budget opens for a 20% increment under the standing rule.

Scale trigger conditions

Required signal
3.5x for 3+ days

Mirage scales once it holds above 3.5x for three to four days with two heroes sharing the load and frequency back under 4x. The core campaign scales once the V3 reallocation lands and the set clears breakeven cleanly. The 20% increment per step stays in place.

The cushion is wide

An 8.16x MER and a CAC to LTV near 1 to 5.7 mean the business has room to keep acquiring aggressively. The constraint on scaling is creative depth and frequency, not the underlying economics, which stayed healthy through a doubled-spend month.

Why mix comes before money

Adding budget to a set carrying a 2.08x ad spreads the problem. Moving that budget to ads already proven above 6x fixes it. June's first gains come from reallocation, and the scaling conversation reopens once the proven sets are the ones holding the spend.

17: Summary

Bottom Line

3.32x Meta ROAS. $19,639 in Shopify gross, a record for the window and up 52.9% on April. 8.16x blended MER. May more than doubled Meta spend to launch Mirage and run Memorial Day at the same time, and the business answered with its strongest revenue month and a 58% jump in new customers.

The wins: Mirage opened at 3.43x, Memorial Day closed at 4.59x, Obsidian held at 4.00x, and the bench (Wife Buyer 9.83x, Skeptic 22.58x, Mirage V3 7.36x) is deep and tested. The drag: Adv+ Winners at 2.08x, held down by V3 Origin Story taking $421.71 at 2.08x, plus a single-creative dependency inside Mirage at 6.22x frequency.

The quarter: spend reset in April rebuilt efficiency, May scaled on top of it at far better unit economics than March. Year on year across April and May, gross sales rose 44.5%, total customers 35.8%, and the returning base 58.7%.

Move the V3 budget to the bench. Build Mirage a second hero. Keep new acquisition climbing. That's the play for June.
