What Q4 customers are worth

Last year's holiday buyers came back least

First-order monthNew customersBought again next monthAvg monthly repeat, months 1 to 6
October 202526614.3%6.8%
November 2025 (BFCM)4167.2%4.5%
December 20253492.3%2.5%
January to March 20262938.2%8.2%

November and December buyers repeat at about half the rate of everyone else. Customers won before the holiday rush are worth more: the case for growing cold now, not leaning on BFCM alone.

Shopify customer cohort analysis, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. Avg monthly repeat = share of the cohort buying in each of months 1 to 6, averaged.

Months 1 to 6 repeat customers summed by month: Oct 2025 108 of 266, Nov 2025 113 of 416, Dec 2025 52 of 349, Jan to Mar 2026 145 of 293. December is mostly gift buying, so the buyer often is not the user. Pooled: Nov and Dec 3.6% a month vs Oct 2025 plus Jan to Mar 2026 7.5%. October's month-1 figure (14.3%) falls in November, so BFCM likely lifted it; the six-month average is the fairer read. The 765 November and December 2025 customers are the core of a BFCM winback audience: one year on from their first order.
