SPEAKER NOTES · 06Cold without BEHIND_08 also fell: 1.47x this week ($84.95 spend, $124.66) against 3.74x last week, so it is not the only cause, but it is the one we control. BEHIND_08 has the highest hook rate in the account (35% in cold this week), so Meta keeps feeding it impressions, but strangers watch and do not buy: 0.82x in cold from 9 to 19 Oct ($183.73, 4 purchases; 0.84x to 18 Oct). Past customers do buy from it: 9.36x in warm over the same span. Lock & Key purchases fell less than cold did (27 vs 30); cold recorded 18 checkouts but 4 purchases, and some of those buyers may have finished under warm credit, though Meta does not report that. That is why cold stays on: it feeds warm, whose reach rose to 4,304 people.
