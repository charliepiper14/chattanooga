July 2026 Monthly Review

July did more with less.
Better returns, lower cost,
on 5% less spend.

Store gross reached $16,876, up 5% on June and up 41% on July last year. Paid did it on less money: spend down 5%, purchases up 11%, Meta ROAS up to 3.63x, and cost per purchase down 15% to $13.31. Blended MER climbed to 8.99x. The month was more efficient than June across the board. The drag underneath it is the same one: nearly half of paid spend still sits on two ads that will not clear the floor.

07 / The August plan

Where we take it next.

Prioritiesordered by impact on purchase volume
01
Reallocate the $891 off the two below-floor ads.
Obsidian Vid 1 at 2.27x and Mirage Desert Lean-In at 2.72x took 47% of July spend under the floor. Cap both. Move the budget to the winners that were starved, Mirage V1 class and the flash creatives at 4.5 to 12x. Same dollars, more purchases. No new spend required.
Cap Vid 1, Desert Lean-InFeed V1, Biggest Sale
02
Refresh Mirage. It is at its reach ceiling.
Frequency 7.67 caps how many new buyers the line can reach no matter the budget. Retire Desert Lean-In and Tropical. Brief fresh Mirage cuts to reopen reach. Keep V1 running but expect it to need new audience soon at frequency 5.60.
Retire Desert Lean-In, TropicalBrief new Mirage
03
Relaunch the ASC with fresh creative, Churchwarden first.
The evergreen is running on Obsidian Vid 1, which has been the biggest-spend under-floor ad for two months straight. Load the new ASC batch: Churchwarden, fresh Mirage and Obsidian scent-identity statics, and re-cut flash winners. June proved a founder video at 5.29x, which backs the founder-led Churchwarden brief.
Launch 6-ad ASC batchLead Churchwarden
04
Reinstate warm retargeting.
June ran a warm retarget at 3.15x. July skipped it. With a 72% returning base plus cart abandoners, a standing Sales campaign to warm audiences is the cheapest incremental volume available, and it runs outside the ASC per the account rules.
Build manual Sales, warmExclude 14d purchasers
05
Stand up an always-on offer to replace the flash.
The flash was 49% of July gross and there is none on the August calendar. A standing incentive, a wash-plus-conditioner bundle or BOGO framed as 50% off the second, keeps flash-style volume flowing without an event. Without it, August volume drifts down as the base can only carry so far.
Offer bundle or 50% off secondGuard protect AOV
On scaling: the blended month clears breakeven, but the evergreen prospecting does not, so budget increases wait until fresh ASC creative holds 2x plus for three to four days, then step 20% at a time. LTV of $83 against an $11 CAC leaves room to bid for volume on new customers below the 3.5x floor when the always-on offer is live.
The month in one line

July was the most efficient month of the quarter, up 41% year over year on less spend. August is about fixing the $891 leak, refreshing Mirage, and replacing the flash with an always-on engine.

Reallocate off the two below-floor ads, refresh Mirage, relaunch the ASC with Churchwarden, reinstate warm retargeting, and stand up the always-on offer. Then step budgets once fresh creative earns it.
